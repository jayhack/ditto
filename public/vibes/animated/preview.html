How we work

002/   MISSION

Spot the signal

We scout breakthrough research or bioinspired tech with clear commercial potential — across mycelium materials, algae catalysts, biomimetic robotics, and regenerative ag.

Build what matters

We partner with founders to develop their venture from the ground up — assembling the right team, designing the product, and validating market, business model, and go-to-market.

Scale and secure

We accelerate the development of the ventures with the right capital and partners — coordinating bridge rounds, regulatory milestones, and the LP introductions that actually convert.

003/   THE STUDIO

Who we
work with

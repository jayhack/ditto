it's a device that

Built around a 10.5-inch Live Paper™ display and a passive EMR stylus, it's the slow-tech device for people who'd rather be sitting in the grass.

LIVE PAPER DISPLAY

We invented a new kind of screen that's brighter than e-ink and faster than LCD — but easier on your eyes than either. It runs at 60fps, so your apps work the way you expect, without the strain.

No eye-strain

Flicker-free display is easy on the eyes.

Extended battery

Days of use on a single charge.

Backlight optional

Read in direct sunlight, no backlight needed.

Display

  • 10.5IN LIVE PAPER™ DISPLAY
    Reflective, sun-readable, paper-like
  • 1600 × 1200 · 190DPI
    Crisp at reading distance
  • OPTIONAL PURE AMBER BACKLIGHT
    For evening reading without blue light
  • DC DIMMING · NO PWM
    Flicker-free at any brightness
  • WACOM EMR PASSIVE STYLUS
    No charging, 4096 pressure levels

  • RAM · 8GB
